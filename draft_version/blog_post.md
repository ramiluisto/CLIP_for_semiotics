# AI-boost to semiotic research

Title says it all - maybe we can use CLIP to automatically rate large collections of images for various keywords like "scary", "warning", "friendly" etc.

## Introduction

With the current boom of chatGPT-hype it is easy to forget that there are a lot of other language and image focused AI-systems that have leapfrogged in the past few years.

With the chatGPT style of intelligent chatbot solutions the well known issue is in hallucinations - the system can make up information. With more narrow systems these issues turn into more manageable statistical-style errors.

We naturally do not suggest that we would turn the analysis of images to the hands of an AI. Due to the way that many of these systems have been trained, we cannot trust them to take into account many of the aspects of semiotics that we take for granted. However, what we can do is to use systems like CLIP to create visualizations and statistical distributions of the AI-systems 'opinions' on images. These can then *guide* us to interesting themes that we might not have spotted from studying only a few images by human eyes.
